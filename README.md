# Omega Hr Insights Dashboard

https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/chanukya.vanjivaka/viz/omega_hr_dashboard/OmegaHRInsightsDashboard

The Omega HR Insights Dashboard is a comprehensive interactive Tableau dashboard designed to streamline and enhance Human Resource (HR) data analysis. Built with a user-centric approach, this dashboard allows HR professionals, analysts, and business leaders to monitor key workforce metrics, visualize trends, and make informed strategic decisions.

This project mimics real-world HR reporting use cases by pulling together fragmented datasets and transforming them into actionable insights through intuitive visualizations.

🔍 Key Functionalities and Features:
📈 Headcount Overview:

Shows current total employees, gender ratio, and department-wise count.

Visualizes workforce composition, helping assess diversity and team balance.

👥 Department-Wise Analysis:

Highlights total number of employees per department.

Allows identification of over- or under-staffed teams, aiding resource allocation.

📉 Attrition & Exit Trends:

Tracks employee exits over time with filters for department, gender, and job role.

Supports churn analysis, helping in retention planning.

📊 Performance Score Distribution:

Analyzes the distribution of employee performance ratings across departments.

Helps management spot patterns in high/low performance, critical for promotions, training, or intervention.

⚙️ Filter Panel (Interactive Slicers):

Allows dynamic filtering by department, gender, or employment status.

Enables custom, role-specific views for different stakeholder needs.

📆 Time Series Analysis:

Shows trends in hiring, attrition, and performance over specific time periods.

Helps in forecasting and planning future HR policies.

💼 Real-World Use Case and Industry Value:
HR Managers can quickly identify departments facing high attrition, address employee dissatisfaction, and assess hiring needs.

Executives & Leadership benefit from real-time workforce insights for quarterly review meetings, diversity analysis, and budget allocation.

Recruiters can analyze historical trends to plan future talent acquisition more efficiently.

Enables fact-based storytelling and eliminates guesswork in HR discussions.

This dashboard mirrors the kind of analytical tools used in top-tier organizations to drive workforce decisions, enhance team productivity, and reduce employee turnover.
